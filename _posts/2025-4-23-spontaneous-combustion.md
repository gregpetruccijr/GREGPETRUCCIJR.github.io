I watched the film [_Don't Worry, He Won't Get Far on Foot_](https://g.co/kgs/gFHAKNe) last night. It tells the recovery story of [John Callahan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Callahan_(cartoonist)). The film shows, quite accurately, what recovery through the 12-Steps of Alcholics Anonymous (AA) looks like. For this alcoholic, that sentiment was underscored in a scene where John's sponsor, Donnie G., says the following: 

> Donnie:
>
>> 1.) Go to meetings
>> 2.) Read the book
>> 3.) Don't drink
>> 4.) Call me before you think you are going to drink, never after.
